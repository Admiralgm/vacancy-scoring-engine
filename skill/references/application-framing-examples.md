## Pattern 1: Beneficiary Data / Identity Coordination Role (stretch-fit)

**Role:** UNICEF Identity Management Interagency Collaboration Consultant
**Score:** 62/100 (viable-but-stretchy)

**Positioning line:**
"Digital identity, interoperability, and operational systems consultant
with recent UNICEF experience and deep African field exposure."

**Lead with:**
- Recent UNICEF consulting experience (GIGA, Learning Passport)
- Identity-linked digital systems — telecom customer data, mobile money,
  MVNO/MVNE, billing, payment systems (BUSPLUS: 500K tx/day, €15M/month)
- Operational guidance production — specs, frameworks, decision-ready docs
- Inter-agency and multi-stakeholder work — UNICEF, governments, WB, IMF
- Africa/emerging-market delivery — 10+ countries, resource-constrained

**De-emphasise:**
- AI agents, LLMs, AI workflows
- Undersea fibre, IRU negotiations, VSAT
- MVNO pioneer (too telecom-specific for this audience)
- Cisco Webex/EdTech COVID deployment

**Bridge arguments:**
- Frame mobile money/payments as identity-linked transactional data systems
  with recipient registration, deduplication risks, fraud controls,
  reconciliation — NOT just payments infrastructure.
- Frame GIGA connectivity work as beneficiary data infrastructure —
  mapping schools and their connectivity creates identity records for
  educational infrastructure beneficiaries.

**Cover letter must:**
Address the education field gap directly by explaining how EE/telecoms
provides the data-systems fluency the role needs, and acknowledge IASC
frameworks as a learning area with demonstrated capacity to absorb UN
operational frameworks quickly.

## Pattern 2: ICT / Digital Infrastructure Role (strong-fit)

**Role:** UNICEF GIGA Program Manager (Infrastructure Finance) P-4
**Score:** 79/100

**Positioning line:**
"Senior digital infrastructure advisor with 25 years operational delivery,
IFI engagement, and direct UNICEF GIGA programme experience."

**Lead with:**
- Direct GIGA Geneva experience (device strategy, connectivity monitoring)
- ITU certified in Financing School Connectivity (blended finance, PPP)
- Project Wakanda ($1B pan-African ISP investment proposal for WB/IMF/AfDB)
- Telecom M&A due diligence ($500M+ combined deal portfolio)
- Operational delivery in 10+ African countries

**De-emphasise:**
- AI agent frameworks (irrelevant to infrastructure finance)
- MVNO/payment systems (distracts from infrastructure narrative)
- COO healthcare experience (Swiss/UN audience won't connect to it)

**Bridge arguments:**
- Frame IRU financial negotiation (SEACOM/EASSy) as IFI/DFI-adjacent deal
  structuring — User owned pricing, terms, and execution with consortium
  counterparties. This is the closest evidence to infrastructure finance
  deal execution in the CV.

**Cover letter must:**
Address the gap between telecom commercial finance and IFI programme-level
infrastructure finance explicitly, positioning the ITU Financing School
Connectivity certification and Project Wakanda as deliberate bridge
experience.

## Pattern 3: AI / Digital Transformation in Development (target-fit)

**Role:** Any AI/Digital Transformation Advisor at UN agency or IFI
**Score:** 75-90 (estimated based on calibration)

**Positioning line:**
"AI practitioner and digital transformation leader deploying production
AI systems in operational environments across Africa and Europe."

**Lead with:**
- Production AI agent deployment (Hermes, OpenClaw with binary patching)
- Local LLM deployment pipeline (Ollama, MLX, quantisation)
- AI-enabled cost reduction (70% Google Ads savings via automated pipeline)
- K-12 AI curriculum design and EdTech programme development
- IFI-facing AI advisory (WB, IMF, AfDB — education & AI integration)
- Transitioned teams from zero AI awareness to daily AI-integrated workflows

**De-emphasise:**
- Telecom/fibre/connectivity infrastructure (only if role is AI-specific)
- COO/P&L operations (unless transformation/change management is the frame)
- Football refereeing (distinctive but not for AI roles)

**Bridge arguments:**
- Frame GIGA/Learning Passport work as "applying AI and digital systems
  to education delivery at scale" — the device procurement and connectivity
  work is the operational foundation that AI systems run on top of.

**Cover letter must:**
Lead with a concrete AI deployment story (Hermes/OpenClaw) rather than
abstract AI interest. The user is "a practitioner who builds AI systems,
not a policy person who talks about AI."
