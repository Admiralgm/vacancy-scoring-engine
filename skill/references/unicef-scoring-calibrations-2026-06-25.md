## Scoring Patterns for UNICEF Consultancies

### Information Management / Data Roles (Remote)
- **P1 cap:** 18 (Data/Analytics/BI) — not 25. These are IM strategy roles, not deep technical data engineering.
- **P2:** 10/15 for consultant (no grade stated). Never assume P-3+ equivalent for ungraded consultancies.
- **P6:** 10/10 for remote work.
- **P7:** 7-8/15 — reasonable match but common role type with many applicants.

### Blockchain / Emerging Tech Roles
- **P1 cap:** 14-16 for blockchain/DLT — not AI/ML cap of 25. Blockchain is a separate domain from AI/agentic systems.
- **P2:** 13/15 for P-3 grade.
- **P7:** 5-6/15 — significant gap if no blockchain experience. Many specialized candidates.

### Knowledge Management Roles
- **P1 cap:** 14 — KM is adjacent to information management but not core ICT. SharePoint/content management is administrative, not technical.
- **P2:** 10/15 for ungraded consultancy.
- **P7:** 6-7/15 — common role, many applicants.

## UNICEF Deadline Format
UNICEF PageUp deadlines are in the format:
```
5 Jul 2026 11:55 PM
```
Convert to tracker format as `2026-07-05`. The 11:55 PM is the standard PageUp cutoff — no INSPIRA-style UTC rollover issue.
