## Lesson: Always Ask About Current Work

The 44-char title scoring missed 14 points because the most relevant current experience (LMS integration) wasn't on the CV at scoring time. The scoring engine's Full-JD Rule is correct, but even a full JD can't capture what the candidate is doing *right now* that isn't yet on their CV.

**For future sessions:** When scoring a role that has a strong "current work" angle, ask the user if they have relevant active work that complements what's in the CV database. This is especially impactful for fast-moving domains (AI, LMS, digital transformation).
