# ChatGPT Inflation Calibration — May 2026 Session

## Summary
Across 4 roles scored in this session, ChatGPT consistently over-scored by ~15-20 points compared to the honest score. The pattern was systematic: over-weighting **thematic/sector alignment** while under-weighting **daily accountability match, grade level, and hard language requirements**.

## Root Cause Pattern
The inflation source was identical across all three cases:
1. **Thematic weighting > daily-accountability weighting.** "UNICEF ✓ + EdTech ✓ + Geneva ✓" drove a high "feels like" score despite no LMS admin experience, no French, and Grade 7 overqualification.
2. **Language requirements treated as soft.** OECD's "knowledge of the other" French requirement was called "not necessarily disqualifying" when it's an explicit screening criterion.
3. **Grade mismatch ignored.** WTO Grade 7 targets 3 years experience. A 30-year COO applying is not a "strong match" — it's an overqualification risk.

## When reviewing external assessments
- If the external assessor leads with "sector alignment" and "mission fit" as primary justification → adjust downward by 15-20
- If the external assessor does not mention grade level, salary, or overqualification risk → the score is likely inflated
- If the external assessor treats language as "not a blocker" when the JD says "knowledge of the other official language" → the score is inflated
- ChatGPT's **framing advice** (positioning lines, "Lead with → De-emphasise" pairs, bridge arguments) is consistently excellent and worth absorbing
